# v2.0.0-beta2 — Account pairing and inverter control (beta)

This is a **beta release** building on the discovery preview from v2.0.0b1. Existing setups keep working exactly as before; the new features below are opt-in.

## What's New

### Connect to your Volcast account

Setup now offers "Connect to your Volcast account" — confirm the pairing in the Volcast app or on the Volcast website, and the window closes by itself. An existing discovery-only or API-key entry can be converted the same way, without losing its history.

### Volcast plan in Home Assistant

Once paired, the integration adds a plan card and a "Volcast" panel showing the current and upcoming plan slots, alongside `sensor.*_control_plan` (the plan) and `sensor.*_control_status` (what the integration is doing with it).

### Prices from a Home Assistant entity

You can point Volcast at your own purchase/sale price sensors instead of (or in addition to) the built-in providers, in **Options → Energy prices**.

### Inverter control through your inverter's integration (beta, off by default)

Volcast can now write plan-driven mode and power settings to your inverter, through your existing inverter integration's entities (for example a select for mode and a number for power). This is **off by default** and needs all three of the following before anything is written:

1. **Through the inverter integration (entities)** selected in **Options → Inverter control**
2. Consent given in the Volcast app
3. The `switch.*_control_switch` entity turned on in Home Assistant

Direct Modbus/local connection to the inverter is not available yet — control in this release only goes through another integration's entities. Automations you already have on your inverter (for example a clock-triggered schedule) are not detected as "someone else took over" — Volcast treats their effect as ordinary drift from the plan, not as a takeover. If you (or another automation) change a controlled setting by hand while the switch is on, Volcast pauses control for 30 minutes, keeps the setting you changed, and raises a repair. When the account revokes consent, the switch is turned off, or the entry is disabled or removed, the last plan-driven setting is put back — except when the owner has meanwhile taken over the mode themselves, in which case Volcast leaves it alone and keeps only the last power setpoint it wrote. Entity-mode control and account pairing are both beta; expect rough edges.

### What is sent after pairing

- Live readings roughly once a minute
- Up to 60 days of past consumption history, sent once after pairing
- Prices from the entity you selected, when configured

Nothing else changes about what discovery already does (see the v2.0.0b1 notes below); pairing does not turn discovery into anything more than a read-only scan.

## What This Does Not Do

- No setting is written to your inverter unless all three control conditions above are met
- No time-window / TOU control in this release — the options screen only previews it
- No direct connection to the inverter; control goes through another Home Assistant integration

## Upgrading and Going Back

Update via HACS. If you'd prefer to revert to v1.7.2, the same steps as in the v2.0.0b1 notes apply — install 1.7.2 and reload; a paired entry keeps working as a plain forecast entry.
