# v2.0.0-beta11 — solar-first charging during grid charge slots

This is a **pre-release** building on v2.0.0-beta10. The change applies only while a grid-charge slot of the plan is active.

## What's New

### Solar surplus during grid charging

- While a grid-charge slot is active and PV production covers the planned charging power, the battery takes the solar surplus instead of the inverter holding a fixed grid-charge power and exporting the surplus.
- If the surplus drops, planned grid charging resumes.
- Applies to inverters whose profile charges from the grid at a fixed power. Currently this is the GoodWe ET family in direct mode.

### Damped switching and write budget

- Switching is damped: about 3 minutes of sustained surplus are required to switch to solar charging, and about 5 minutes below the planned power to switch back.
- Switching uses at most half of the daily mode-write budget of the inverter, leaving the rest for the plan.
- It is never active during the device verification test window.

### Visibility

- The control telemetry shows when solar-first charging is used, as a `pv_first` note in the decision.

### Internal

- Battery power is now part of the live telemetry used by the control cycle.

## Upgrading

Update via HACS and restart Home Assistant.
