# v2.0.0-beta17 — Volcast 2.0 beta

This is a **pre-release** for testers of the Volcast 2.0 app. It replaces all earlier 2.0.0 betas. The stable line
is unchanged; stay on it unless you were invited to the 2.0 beta.

## What's new

- **Nightly reconciliation after a data gap** — hours covered by a reading sent after a data gap are no longer re-sent by the nightly reconciliation, so they keep the value reported for the whole gap instead of being overwritten by a partial one. No action is required.
- **Sensor assignment proposal** — when Volcast does not recognise your inverter integration, live data does not flow until you pick the sensors. Volcast now builds a proposal from your Home Assistant sensors and the recorder history and shows it in **Options → Installation details**, with the sensors prefilled. Review every sensor and submit; nothing is saved until you do, and no inverter setting is changed. Suggestions are only offered when no inverter integration is recognised.
- **Repair for a missing assignment** — a paired entry without a recognised profile, a direct connection or a PV power sensor now raises the repair "Volcast needs to know which sensors to use". Its "Choose your inverter sensors" step shows the same proposal and needs a PV power sensor before it closes. The repair is not saved once a profile is recognised, and not offered while a newly delivered profile waits for a reload.
- **Sign check** — Volcast expects battery power to be positive while discharging and grid power to be positive while importing. The proposal compares your sensors over the last 24 hours of recorder statistics (current readings when there is no history) and tells you when a sign looks reversed. It needs a house load sensor; without one there is no recommendation, and a single load, a vehicle or a partial sensor is never treated as certain.
- **Battery sign option** — "Battery sensor shows discharging as negative" in "Installation details" flips the sign of a battery power sensor you chose by hand (it does not apply to the sensors of a recognised integration). Changing this option does not reload the integration.
- **Read-only service** — `volcast.propose_sensor_assignment` returns the proposal and the sign check for use in automations. It changes nothing; the optional `config_entry_id` picks the entry when several are paired.

## Fixes

- **False warning about the verification record** — an empty stored device verification record is no longer logged as malformed at start-up.
- **Pairing warning in Home Assistant** — the external pairing step now passes its step id, which removes a warning that future Home Assistant versions would turn into an error.
- **Update notice** — the hint now names both HACS and the Home Assistant Marketplace.
- **Newly delivered profiles** — the notice no longer calls a profile delivered through the profile feed a "test profile", and says accurately when such a profile can lead to inverter writes.
- **Translations** — the new texts are translated into all supported languages.

## Upgrading from an earlier 2.0.0 beta

Update in HACS and restart Home Assistant. Your forecast entities and history are kept.
