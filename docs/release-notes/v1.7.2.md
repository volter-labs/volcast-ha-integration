# v1.7.2 — Battery charge power carried across quiet hours

Small fix release for installations with a battery.

## Production tracking

- **Charge power no longer disappears when the battery is full** — Home Assistant only reports a sensor when its value changes, so a battery sitting at 0 W for hours produced no readings at all. Those hours were sent without the battery charge power, and the backend could not tell a full-battery curtailment apart from a cloudy afternoon. The integration now keeps the last known charge power and reuses it for hours without a state change, the same way it already handled the state of charge. No setup changes needed; the fix applies automatically to the next hour after updating.

## Why it matters

Hours where the inverter throttles because the battery is full and export is limited are now recognised as curtailed. They are shown as such in the app and excluded from calibration, so the forecast stops learning from throttled afternoons.
